Design–build under one roof

Why Alvarez

One crew, one number, one standard — from first sketch to the day you get the keys.

Most projects go sideways in the handoffs — architect to contractor, contractor to sub, sub to whoever. We keep design and build under the same roof, so nothing gets lost in translation and no one gets to point fingers.

  • A single point of contact from day one
  • Fixed-price transparency — the number you sign is the number you pay
  • In-house craftsmen, not a rotating cast of subs
  • Permits, inspections and paperwork handled for you
  • Backed by a 10-year workmanship warranty
