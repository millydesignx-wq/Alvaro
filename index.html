The approach

One crew, one number, one standard.

Most builds go sideways in the handoffs — architect to contractor, contractor to sub, sub to whoever's free. We keep design and construction under one roof, so nothing gets lost in translation and no one gets to point fingers.

  • A single point of contact.One person owns your project from the first walk to the final punch list.
  • Fixed-price transparency.The number you sign is the number you pay. Change orders in writing, or not at all.
  • In-house craftsmen.Our own carpenters and finishers — not a rotating cast of the cheapest subs available.
  • A 10-year workmanship warranty.We answer the phone long after the last invoice clears.

The process

Four stages. No surprises.

Signed off at every line — you always know exactly where things stand.

Stage 01

Consult

We walk the space, learn how you'll use it, and give you a straight answer on scope and budget — free, no pressure.

Stage 02

Design

Plans, materials, and a fixed price you can build to. You approve every line before a tool comes out.

Stage 03

Build

One dedicated crew, a clean site, and weekly updates so you always know where things stand.

Stage 04

Handover

A final walkthrough, every detail dialed in, and a 10-year warranty in your hands. Then we get out of your way.
